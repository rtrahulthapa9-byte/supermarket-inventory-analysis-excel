# supermarket-inventory-analysis-excel

**Duration:** January 2024 to June 2024

**Report Objective:** This dashboard provides an overview of inventory levels, sales performance, stock availability, and Average delivery to support inventory monitoring and operational decision-making.

**Executive Summary:** January to July 2024, total sales value reached  ₹26.3 Million. The report also highlights current inventory value, stock availability, and products requiring attention due to low-stock levels. 

**KPI Summary**
* Stock Value
* Stock Quantity
* Unit Sold
* Sales Value
* Average Delivery
  
**Visualization**
* Stock Category wise
* Sales Category Wise
* Monthly Sales Trend
* Monthly Sold Units
* Low Stock Product Wise
  
**Key Insights**
* Meat (11.6%) and Beverages (11.4%) hold the most stock over the last 6 months.
* 49.9% of 1,049.25K units sold; ₹2.64 crore tied up in inventory. 
* April is the peak sales month (₹4.5M)
* Popcorn leads low-stock alerts (33), Followed by Soda and Fish Sticks (30 each)
* The highest number of units was sold in April

**Conclusion:** Overall, the report provides visibility into inventory levels, sales performance, and stock availability. The identified low-stock products and inventory levels should be monitored regularly to support smooth operations.
